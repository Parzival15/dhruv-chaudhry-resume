🏆 The signature feature

Ranking that actually means something.

Forget agonizing over whether something's a 7 or an 8. You just bucket a movie 👍 liked · 🙂 fine · 👎 disliked, then Reelo asks a few quick "which did you like more?" questions.

  • A handful of quick comparisons finds exactly where a film slots into your list — in just a few taps.
  • Your ranking derives a 0–10 score per movie, shown as an at-a-glance coin colored red → amber → green.
  • Scores stay relative: rank one movie higher and everything below it re-balances automatically.
👥 Built to be social

A feed of what your people are watching.

Every ranking becomes a post. Follow friends and critics you trust, and your feed fills with real reactions — not algorithmic noise.

  • Follow anyone, then flip between an Everyone and a Following feed.
  • Like & comment on any log, with a threaded discussion per movie.
  • Rich profiles with avatars, watch counts, and your full ranked list.
▶ For You

Movie scenes, one swipe at a time.

A vertical, swipeable feed of iconic movie clips. Discover your next watch the way you already scroll — then log it without leaving the reel.

  • Clips play as you scroll — tap once to bring the sound in.
  • Like & comment right from the clip, and add it to your watchlist.
  • You never see the same clip twice, with an "all caught up" finish.
🎨 AI Taste Palette

Your taste, as a color palette.

Reelo reads the colors of your highest-rated films and generates a personal mood board — a shareable card that captures the aesthetic of your taste.

  • A one-of-a-kind palette and a poetic name drawn from your favorite films.
  • A polished share-card with a "winners' podium" of your best-rated movies.
  • One tap to save it or share it with friends.

How it works

From "just watched it" to ranked in three taps.

1

Search & log

Find any movie in a huge catalog and mark it watched — or save it to your Want-to-Watch list.

2

Rank it head-to-head

Pick 👍 / 🙂 / 👎, then answer a couple of quick head-to-heads. Reelo slots it in and scores it 0–10.

3

Follow & discover

Follow friends, react to their logs, browse For-You reels, and get recommendations tuned to your rankings.
